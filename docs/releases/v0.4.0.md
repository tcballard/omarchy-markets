# A clearer watchlist. A chart when you want it.

Markets v0.4.0 makes your watchlist easier to scan and lets you fold the chart away when a quick glance is enough.

![Omarchy Markets v0.4.0 running on an XPS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tcballard/omarchy-markets/v0.4.0/preview.png)

## What changed

- **More room to read.** Company names sit beneath symbols, prices line up, and the selected quote has a larger chart.
- **Your choice of detail.** Use **Hide chart / Show chart**, or press **D**, to switch between the watchlist and full details. Markets remembers your choice until the plugin reloads.
- **Clearer comparisons.** Daily change is measured against the previous close. Chart return is measured from the first plotted price, with a matching baseline.
- **A tidier panel.** Range controls sit beside the chart. The data-source footer stays inside the panel while the content scrolls.

Your watchlists, pinned symbol and ticker settings carry over.

## Update

```bash
omarchy plugin update io.github.tcballard.omarchy-markets --yes &&
omarchy restart shell
```

New to Markets? [Start with the README](https://github.com/tcballard/omarchy-markets#install).
